Two representations of the same photograph

Not the agent, and not the bank above. This looks the same image up twice over the bank rows that have tiles cached — once as 3 crops, which is what ships, and once as 3 crops + 6 tiles level-weighted. Same images, same width, so a difference is the representation and nothing else. Measured on OSV-5M's own split, adding the tiles is +4.5 pp at 25 km on sequence and +0.8 pp on the geographic holdout.
